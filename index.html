1. Drum Machine & Sound Pads

Tap a pad to play its sound. Use A–L on a computer keyboard for quick performance.

Tap piano keys to play notes. The piano note rows below can also be programmed into your song.

🔴 Record & Download Your Song

Start recording, play your song, then stop recording to prepare a downloadable audio file. Recording captures the mixed sound output in real time, so let the song play for as long as you want to record.

🎙️ Live Voice Input Microphone off

Allow microphone access when your browser asks. Then speak, sing, rap, or record ad-libs while the beat plays. Your voice is mixed into the same recording as the drums, piano, bass, and synth. Use headphones to prevent the speakers feeding back into the microphone.

⬇ Download Audio Not recording

Audio format depends on your browser (usually WebM or Ogg). “Save Project JSON” saves your pattern and tempo, not audio. For vocals: enable the microphone, press Start Recording, press Play Song, perform your voice, then Stop Recording and download. Use headphones for a cleaner vocal mix.
